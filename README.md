# Department Records Desk

Internal repository used by the Department of Urban Systems to keep the
records review trail in one place. It holds:

- `records/intake/` - documents staged by the front office, awaiting filing.
- `records/study-applications/` - material relating to admission to study programmes.
- `records/work-applications/` - material relating to employment and recruitment.
- `records/general-records/` - facilities, supplier and finance paperwork.
- `docs/AUDIT_REFERENCE.md` - the reference index for the desk automation.
- `tools/` - the automation scripts used during the quarterly review.

Filing rules are maintained by the records officer. The intake folder is
emptied at the end of every review cycle.
